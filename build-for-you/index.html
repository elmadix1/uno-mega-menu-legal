How it works

Five steps. Your part takes about fifteen minutes.

  1. You choose a tier and email me. The button opens a message with the few things I need to know. I reply within one business day with a short brief and a secure payment link.
  2. You pay and approve my access. Shopify sends you my access request, limited to apps and themes. You approve it in one click and can remove it at any time.
  3. I build on a copy of your theme. Your live store does not change while I work.
  4. You review the preview. I send you a private link. You send all your changes in one message.
  5. I put it live. I switch the menu on in your live theme, hide your old menu and check every link on desktop and phone.

Questions

The answers merchants ask for before ordering.

Who builds my menu?

I do. I am Mohamed, the developer of Uno Mega Menu. I am based in Mauritius (GMT+4) and I reply within one business day.

When does the delivery time start?

When I have three things: your payment, your approved access and your completed brief. Business days are Monday to Friday.

What do you need from me?

The items you want in your navigation, the collections and products to feature, your brand colors and any banner images. Images are supplied by you, in the size I give you in the brief. If you are not sure about the structure, tell me what you sell and I will propose one.

Do I have to install the app first?

Yes. Install Uno Mega Menu from the Shopify App Store, it takes a minute and the Free plan costs nothing. For Business and Signature, start the free trial of the plan shown on the tier.

How many products can the menu show?

Shopify lets a menu show up to 20 hand-picked products with live price and buy button on a page. For larger selections I use collection columns, which have no such limit and always show the current products of the collection.

Will it work with my theme?

Yes on every Online Store 2.0 theme, which covers Dawn, Horizon and almost every theme sold today. Custom and older themes are handled on quote.

What is a revision?

One batch of changes sent in a single message, within 14 days of delivery. Changes that go beyond the tier you ordered are quoted before I start.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, as long as I have not started building. Once the work has started, the included revisions are there to get the menu right.

What can you see in my store?

Apps and themes only. I cannot see your orders, your customers or your finances. I do not edit your theme files either: the menu is an app block that disappears if you uninstall the app.

Can I edit the menu myself afterwards?

Yes. Everything is built in the app's visual editor, so you can change a link, a product or a color yourself at any time. Monthly care is there if you would rather not.
