Privacy Policy

Uno Mega Menu · Effective July 26, 2026

Uno Mega Menu ("the app") helps Shopify merchants build navigation menus for their storefront. This policy explains what the app stores, why it stores it and how it is protected.

Information the app stores

  • Shop identification. The myshopify domain of your store together with the access tokens Shopify issues during installation. These are required for the app to operate inside your admin.
  • Menu configuration. The menus, designs, badges, saved menus, uploaded font references and settings you create in the builder. This configuration is stored in a metafield on your own shop.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store or process personal data about your customers. It does not read orders, customer profiles or checkout data. It contains no advertising trackers.

How information is used

Stored data is used for one purpose only: rendering your menu in the builder and on your storefront. If you use the Auto-translate feature, the text of your menu is sent to Google Cloud Translation API to generate the translation. Beyond that, nothing is ever sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Where information lives

Session data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway. Menu configuration lives in Shopify metafields on your own store. When Auto-translate is used, menu text is also sent to Google Cloud Translation API for processing; see Google's own privacy policy for how they handle it. Traffic between your browser, Shopify and the app is encrypted with TLS.

Data retention and deletion

When you uninstall the app, Shopify revokes its access and the app deletes the associated session data. Shop data removal requests sent by Shopify through the mandatory compliance webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact) are honored automatically. You can also request deletion at any time by email.

Your rights

You can ask what data the app holds about your shop, request a copy or request deletion. Write to the address below; requests are answered within 30 days.
