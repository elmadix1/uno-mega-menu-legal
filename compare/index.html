Are they still true?

Menu apps put products in front of shoppers in one of two ways. They copy your product data once, or they read it live on every page view. That single design choice decides whether your menu can lie.

The snapshot approach

Most menu builders store a copy of each product you pick: its name, its image, its price. The copy is taken the day you build the menu. From that day on, your store moves and the copy does not. Lower a price, launch a sale or sell out a size and the menu keeps showing the world as it was.

This is not a bug in those apps. It is the design. A copied number has no way to update itself.

The live approach

Uno renders your menu on Shopify's servers, inside your storefront, at the moment each page loads. Product cards read the current price, the current stock and the current promotion straight from your store. There is no copy to go stale, so there is nothing to resync and nothing to remember.
