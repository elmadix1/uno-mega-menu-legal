FAQ

Good questions

Will it work with my theme?

Yes. Uno is a theme app block that you add to your header from the theme editor, which works with every Online Store 2.0 theme. On older themes the block is not added automatically and you add it yourself from the block picker. The app walks you through both cases.

Will it slow my store down?

No. The menu is rendered on Shopify's servers together with your page, in the same request. There is no external app server in the path, no JavaScript framework and no flash of unstyled menu while something loads. The assets are one small stylesheet and one small script.

What happens if my prices or stock change?

The menu follows, on the next page view. Product cards read live data from your store: current price, sale price struck through, price ranges across variants, sold out states and per-market currency conversion. Nothing is copied, so nothing goes stale.

I see two menus. Is that normal?

Yes, right after activation your theme's original menu is still there. You hide it with a small CSS snippet that the app gives you, with the exact path showing where to paste it. It takes about a minute.

What happens if I uninstall?

The menu block disappears with the app. Uno does not inject code into your theme files, so there is nothing to clean up. Your original theme menu is still where it always was.

Can I try a paid plan first?

Every paid plan has a 14-day free trial and the Free plan has no time limit. You can also build and test everything on a development store before touching your live theme.
